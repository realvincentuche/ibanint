# Commercial bus imagery

These are illustrative photos of Chinese-built Yutong buses, not photographs of IBAN International vehicles. Both were taken at Busworld Europe 2025 and are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Photographer and license information is available through the page's collapsed Image credits disclosure; it also notes that the images are cropped for display.

- `logistics-yutong-double-decker.jpg`: [Yutong U11DD double-deck bus](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Busworld_Europe_2025,_Brussels_(20251003-P1052212).jpg), photo by Matti Blume. Downloaded Wikimedia thumbnail, 1920 × 2560.
- `logistics-yutong-coach.jpg`: [Yutong T14E commercial coach](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Busworld_Europe_2025,_Brussels_(P1210645).jpg), photo by Celestinesucess. Downloaded Wikimedia thumbnail, 1920 × 1440.
