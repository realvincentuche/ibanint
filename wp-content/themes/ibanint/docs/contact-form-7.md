# Contact Form 7 setup

Create a form in Contact Form 7 and paste this into its **Form** tab. Copy the shortcode generated by Contact Form 7 into the WordPress **Contact Us** page content. The theme places that page content below the introduction in the first column of the Start Here section.

```html
<p><label>Full name
[text* your-name autocomplete:name]</label></p>

<p><label>Email address
[email* your-email autocomplete:email]</label></p>

<p><label>Phone or WhatsApp (optional)
[tel your-phone autocomplete:tel]</label></p>

<p><label>Organization (optional)
[text your-organization]</label></p>

<p><label>Business area
[select* your-sector first_as_label "Select a business area" "International & Commodity Trade" "Energy" "Agriculture & Food" "Transportation & Logistics" "Infrastructure Advisory" "Real Estate Development" "Waste Management" "Mining & Natural Resources" "Other"]</label></p>

<p><label>Your message
[textarea* your-message]</label></p>

<p>[acceptance privacy-consent] I have read the <a href="https://ibanint.com/privacy-policy/">Privacy Policy</a> and agree to be contacted about my inquiry. [/acceptance]</p>

<p>[submit "Send inquiry"]</p>
```

Keep the standard CF7 submit tag. The theme gives it the same square gold style and right-arrow icon as the site buttons.

In the **Mail** tab, use:

```text
To: info@ibanint.com
From: IBAN International <info@ibanint.com>
Subject: New website inquiry: [your-sector]
Additional headers: Reply-To: [your-email]

Name: [your-name]
Email: [your-email]
Phone: [your-phone]
Organization: [your-organization]
Business area: [your-sector]

Message:
[your-message]
```

Use the shortcode Contact Form 7 generates for your form. The plugin is not installed in this local project, so sending a test inquiry requires the live plugin and mail delivery to be configured.
