IBAN International Ltd operates ibanint.com. This policy explains how we handle information sent through the website and information needed to run it.

Information we receive

If you contact us by form, email or phone, we receive the details you choose to provide. These may include your name, email address, phone number, organization, business area and message. Our hosting service may also record technical details such as your IP address, browser and the time of a request in server logs.

How we use it

We use inquiry details to respond, discuss a possible business relationship and keep a record of the conversation. We use technical logs to keep the website running and investigate errors or misuse. We do not sell personal information.

Who handles the information

People working on behalf of IBAN International may see information needed to answer an inquiry. Website hosting and email providers process information to deliver these services. We may disclose information when required by law. Because we work across regions, a conversation or service provider may involve processing information outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to answer them and manage any resulting business relationship or record-keeping obligation. Hosting providers retain technical logs under their own service settings. You can ask about a particular record by contacting us.

Cookies and outside services

This theme does not set advertising or analytics cookies. WordPress may use cookies for authorized administrators who log in. Videos and fonts in the theme are hosted with the website. If we add analytics, embedded media or other services that use cookies, we will update this policy and seek consent where required.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us, subject to records we need to keep. To make a request or ask a privacy question, email info@ibanint.com.

Changes

We will update this page when our website or data practices change. Last updated: 2 November 2026.
